The Firm

Where law meets strategy.

Nex Legal is a team. Every file is read by a partner, every strategy is settled together with the associates who will carry it, and the whole team remains answerable for every result.

The firm operates from its head office in Defence Colony and its branch office in Greater Kailash I, both in South Delhi and within easy reach of the Supreme Court, the High Court of Delhi, the NCLT and NCLAT benches, the Debts Recovery Tribunals and the district courts at Saket and Patiala House. That lets us attend urgent mentions, inspect records and file at short notice.

The team has acted for public institutions, statutory regulators, insurance corporations, banks and financial institutions, liquidators and corporate entities, and for individual litigants in sensitive civil, family and criminal matters. Working on both sides helps us anticipate how the other side and the bench will view a case. In matters of significance we brief senior counsel of standing, always in consultation with the client.

Law.

We read the statute, the record and the precedent closely, and give advice in plain words that a client can act on.

Strategy.

We build the case around what the client actually needs: the right forum, the right relief and the right moment to press or to settle.

Resolution.

We measure ourselves by the outcome we secure, not by the length of the proceedings we conduct.

Counsel across the courts and tribunals of New Delhi

Special leave petitions, civil and criminal appeals, transfer petitions and statutory appeals from tribunals such as the NCLAT and APTEL.

RERA complaints and appeals, title investigation, conveyancing, land and tenancy litigation, and development and construction disputes.

Divorce, maintenance, custody, domestic violence proceedings, stridhan recovery and transfer petitions before the Supreme Court.

Labour court and High Court proceedings on termination, wages and gratuity, and service matters including compassionate appointment.

Insurance Litigation

Motor accident claims, insurance recovery and repudiation disputes, and appeals before the High Court and the consumer commissions.

Advisory & Data Protection

Legal opinions, contract review, legal notices, pre-institution mediation, and advice under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Our Team

One team, from the first brief to the final order

Nex Legal works as a single team. Each matter is led by a partner, run day to day by a principal associate, and carried through court by associates, research and registry. Clients deal with the whole team, not a single individual.

I

Tier One

Partners

The partners set the strategy, settle every important draft, argue the significant hearings and answer for the outcome. Each matter has a partner assigned to it from the day it is accepted.

II

Tier Two

Principal Associates

Principal associates are the senior advocates of the team below the partners. They run matters day to day, lead the drafting, appear in the courts and tribunals, and are the client's first point of contact between hearings.

How We Work

From the first brief to the final order

The same standards apply to every engagement, whether it is a single hearing or a portfolio of matters.

  1. The brief is received by the team

    Every new matter is acknowledged within one working day, with the names of the responsible partner and associate.

  2. A preliminary assessment

    Within three working days we set out the merits, the risks, the likely timeline and the estimated costs. Urgent matters are assessed the same day.

  3. Drafts shared before filing

    Pleadings and applications are shared for the client's approval well before filing. Urgent filings are made within forty-eight hours of complete instructions.

  4. A report after every hearing

    On the day of each hearing the client receives a note of what transpired, the next date and any action required.

  5. Ongoing review

    Institutional clients receive a consolidated matter tracker every month and a review of their portfolio every quarter.

Professional Conduct

Confidentiality and conflicts

The firm practises in accordance with the Advocates Act, 1961 and the Bar Council of India Rules.

A conflict check is carried out before any mandate is accepted. Client information is held on access-controlled systems, shared only with the team working on the matter, and handled in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

The firm does not refer to any client or matter in publicity of any kind.

Out-of-pocket expenses are charged at actuals against receipts, and senior counsel fees are agreed with the client before any briefing.
