The Firm

Where law meets strategy.

Nex Legal is led by its partners, Kunal Singh and Mahi Sharma. A partner reads every file, settles every strategy and remains answerable for every result.

The firm operates from its head office in Defence Colony and its branch office in Greater Kailash I, both in South Delhi and within easy reach of the Supreme Court, the High Court of Delhi, the NCLT and NCLAT benches, the Debts Recovery Tribunals and the district courts at Saket and Patiala House. That lets us attend urgent mentions, inspect records and file at short notice.

Our partners and associates have acted for public institutions, regulators, lenders and liquidators, and for private parties in sensitive civil, family and criminal matters. Working on both sides helps us anticipate how the other side and the bench will view a case. In matters of significance we brief senior counsel of standing, always in consultation with the client.

Law.

We read the statute, the record and the precedent closely, and give advice in plain words that a client can act on.

Strategy.

We build the case around what the client actually needs: the right forum, the right relief and the right moment to press or to settle.

Resolution.

We measure ourselves by the outcome we secure, not by the length of the proceedings we conduct.

Practice Areas

Counsel across the courts and tribunals of New Delhi

Each area may be engaged on its own or as part of a wider mandate, with the same partner supervision throughout.

Supreme Court & Appellate

Special leave petitions, civil and criminal appeals, transfer petitions and statutory appeals from tribunals such as the NCLAT and APTEL.

Commercial Litigation & Arbitration

Commercial suits, summary suits and injunctions; institutional and ad hoc arbitration, interim measures, challenges to awards and enforcement.

Recovery, Enforcement & Insolvency

SARFAESI, DRT and DRAT proceedings, Section 138 complaints, execution of decrees, and creditor and debtor work before the NCLT and NCLAT.

Consumer Protection

Complaints and defence before the District, State and National Consumer Commissions, including product liability claims.

Real Estate & RERA

RERA complaints and appeals, title investigation, conveyancing, land and tenancy litigation, and development and construction disputes.

Intellectual Property

Trade mark and copyright enforcement, infringement and passing off suits, local commissions, and action against online infringement.

Competition & Regulatory

Proceedings before the Competition Commission of India and sectoral regulators, and writ challenges to regulations and executive action.

Criminal & White Collar

Anticipatory and regular bail, quashing petitions, complaints for cheating and criminal breach of trust, and trial representation.

Family & Matrimonial

Divorce, maintenance, custody, domestic violence proceedings, stridhan recovery and transfer petitions before the Supreme Court.

Employment & Service

Labour court and High Court proceedings on termination, wages and gratuity, and service matters including compassionate appointment.

Technology & Data Protection

Advice on the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, technology contracts and emerging questions of AI and the law.

Advisory & Pre-Litigation

Legal opinions, contract review, legal notices and replies, and pre-institution mediation under the Commercial Courts Act.
